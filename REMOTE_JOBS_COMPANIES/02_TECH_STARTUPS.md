## 📝 How to Apply to Startups

1. **Check funding stage** - Series A-C are most stable
2. **Research leadership** - Google founders and background
3. **Review culture** - Check Glassdoor reviews
4. **Understand equity** - Ask about stock options, vesting
5. **Prepare for growth** - Startups scale rapidly
6. **Network** - Often hire through referrals
7. **Be flexible** - Roles may evolve

## 💡 Startup vs MNC Comparison

| Factor | Startup | MNC |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Growth | Fast | Steady |
| Flexibility | High | Lower |
| Equity | Higher % | Lower % |
| Salary | Varies | Stable |
| Benefits | Basic | Comprehensive |
| Job Security | Lower | Higher |
| Work-Life | Often harder | Better balance |
| Learning | Rapid | Structured |
