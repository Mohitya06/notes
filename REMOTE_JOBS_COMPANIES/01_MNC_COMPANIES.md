## 📋 Application Tips

1. **Research thoroughly** - Understand company culture & values
2. **Tailor resume** - Highlight relevant experience
3. **Practice interviews** - Use LeetCode, HackerRank
4. **Prepare examples** - Have STAR method stories ready
5. **Network** - Connect with employees on LinkedIn
6. **Follow up** - Polite follow-up after applications

---

## 💡 Salary Negotiation Tips

1. Research market rates for your role/location
2. Highlight your value and achievements
3. Ask for range, don't give first number
4. Consider total compensation (stocks, benefits)
5. Be prepared to walk away
6. Get offer in writing before accepting
