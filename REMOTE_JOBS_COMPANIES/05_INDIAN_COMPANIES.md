## 🎯 Interview Process (Indian Companies)

### Typical stages:
1. **Online Test** - Coding, aptitude, English
2. **Technical Round 1** - Data structures, algorithms
3. **Technical Round 2** - System design, projects
4. **HR Round** - Culture fit, salary negotiation

### Preparation tips:
- Practice on LeetCode/HackerRank
- Study system design
- Prepare project details
- Research company thoroughly
- Practice behavioral questions
