## 💡 Interview Success Tips

### What Interviewers Want
✅ Problem-solving skills
✅ Communication ability
✅ Knowledge of fundamentals
✅ Real-world experience
✅ Learning mindset

### What Interviewers Don't Want
❌ Memorized answers
❌ Overcomplication
❌ Lack of clarity
❌ No questions at end
❌ Arrogance

### During Interview
1. **Listen carefully** - Make sure you understand before coding
2. **Ask questions** - Clarify requirements and edge cases
3. **Think aloud** - Explain your approach
4. **Test your code** - Run through examples
5. **Optimize gradually** - Get working solution first
6. **Mention tradeoffs** - Show you understand complexity
7. **Ask follow-up questions** - Show interest and curiosity
